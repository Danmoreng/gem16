## N1 unprofiled full-model results

ABBA used three warmups and ten retained samples per cohort on the AP1 16K workload.
The D2 trace confirms 15,060 to 1,004 table launches and the same 15,060 epilogues.
Other kernel shapes/resources, 1,135 outputs, 1,004 proposals and 90 layer boundaries match.
The isolated replay passes memcheck and synccheck. Both measured modes pass prefill and TTFT.
Sampled peak VRAM is 12,725 MiB for both D2 binaries and 12,455 MiB for both Ordinary binaries.

The Ordinary intervals include zero and satisfy the −0.20% lower-bound component.
Both point estimates miss the nonnegative requirement. The candidate was rejected.
The remaining acceptance-only protected profile and lifecycle checks were skipped.

## Other screens

- N3 reproduces 90 real router outputs. Both geometries use 36 registers and zero local storage.
  The three isolated 30-layer windows lose time. Integrated fork/join timing was not measured.
- N2 reproduces 12 real O outputs after stream-ordered diagnostic upload was fixed.
  It emits an actual 64-bit weight load and validates the inverse byte permutation.
  Cache pressure is modeled by a matched 96 MiB touch. The production format was not migrated.
- N4 reproduces entire cache and sentinel bytes for actual 0/1/2 acceptance and nine
  additional boundary cases. The three acceptance classes are weighted by the 502-group histogram.
  The isolated replay omits surrounding layers. Production lifecycle work was not reached.
- N5 verifies all 65,536 codebook states and 12 actual K3/K4 GateUp output vectors.
  Its weighted estimate comes from warm operator replay, with equal case weights times 30 layers.
  The average of case medians goes from 62.52 to 197.01 µs per projection.
  Disassembly confirms byte loads in the candidate.

No candidate qualified. There is no new cross-engine comparison in this record.
