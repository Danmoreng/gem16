# 12B DFlash rejected; external MTP depth references retained

DFlash is closed after the owner decision of 17 November 2026: it was slower
than each engine's existing MTP D2. Both its BF16 GGUF and original Safetensors
weights have been deleted (2.926 GB total); no other model referenced the
original cache blob. Target and Google MTP Assistant weights are unaffected.
Conversion/start helpers, failed-attempt logs and other temporary files were
removed. There is no DFlash runtime integration or pending optimization task.

The measurements below are historical 16 November cohorts. MTP D3/D4 uses the
Google Assistant, not DFlash, and remains useful as an external reference.

| Engine | Method | Drafts | Warmups + retained | Decode tok/s | Engine D2 reference |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| llama-cpp | dflash | 15 | 3 + 3 | 66.50 | 97.20 |
| llama-cpp | dflash | 8 | 1 + 3 | 72.49 | 97.20 |
| llama-cpp | mtp | 3 | 1 + 3 | 101.26 | 97.20 |
| llama-cpp | mtp | 4 | 1 + 3 | 103.10 | 97.20 |
| vllm | dflash | 15 | 3 + 10 | 78.51 | 112.83 |
| vllm | dflash | 8 | 1 + 3 | 80.03 | 112.83 |
| vllm | mtp | 3 | 1 + 3 | 117.41 | 112.83 |
| vllm | mtp | 4 | 1 + 3 | 117.29 | 112.83 |

Rates are medians; existing D2 values were reused, not freshly controlled.
The llama.cpp 15-draft run completed three warmups and was stopped after six
retained requests; the first three chronological samples supply its statistic,
and all six remain in JSONL. Subsequent 8-draft/D3/D4 cohorts use one warmup
and three retained requests. The earlier vLLM 15-draft cohort uses 3 + 10.
No three-draft DFlash or extra depth sweep was run.

Target formats/KV and output streams differ across engines. These cohorts
do not establish token-ID or quality equivalence across engines. Original
samples, output IDs, telemetry, stop metadata and verification are retained,
in the [original run directory](https://github.com/Danmoreng/gem16/tree/9f7f7a6555ac169a9e49580f11824eef0e55fd85/artifacts/dflash/2026-11-16). The folder is historical
evidence only; it contains no model weights or runnable DFlash helper.
